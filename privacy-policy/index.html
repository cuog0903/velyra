Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VELYRA Studio collects, uses and protects information when you use our mobile game Bowl of Pho.

Information We Collect

Bowl of Pho does not require users to create an account. We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or home address.

Game Data

The game may store local gameplay information on your device, such as your progress or high scores, to improve your gaming experience. This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Advertising

Bowl of Pho displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob, including rewarded video ads and interstitial ads. To serve these ads, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process data such as your Advertising ID, device information, IP address, and app usage data, which may be used to show personalised or non-personalised ads depending on your consent choices. If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we will ask for your consent before showing personalised ads, in accordance with Google's EU User Consent Policy. You can review or change your choice at any time through the consent management option available in the app. For more information about how Google uses data when you use our app, please see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Children's Privacy

Bowl of Pho does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Third-Party Services

Bowl of Pho uses the following third-party services: Google AdMob — serves in-app advertisements (see "Advertising" above). Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — manages user consent for advertising in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland. Firebase Analytics — collects anonymous, aggregated statistics about how the game is played (for example, session length and gameplay events), to help us understand and improve the game. Firebase Crashlytics — collects crash reports and diagnostic information if the app encounters an error, to help us identify and fix bugs. These services may collect data such as device information, IP address, and app usage data. None of these services are used to collect your name, email address, phone number, or home address. You can find more information about how Google handles data through these services in Google's Privacy Policy.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
